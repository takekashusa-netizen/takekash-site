HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. You only do the first one.

QUESTIONS
How long does it take?

Most streams come back the same day. Long streams can take up to 24 hours.

What links work?

Twitch VODs and highlights, Kick VODs, and YouTube videos or past livestreams, 10 minutes or longer.

Do I need an account?

No. Just your stream link and an email to send the clips to.

Who owns the clips?

You do. Only send videos you have the rights to.

Is there a watermark?

Every clip carries a small “Made by TakeKash” tag in the top corner, clear of the app buttons and your captions.
