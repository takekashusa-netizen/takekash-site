How the quote is worked out

  • Editing time is hours × your hourly rate.
  • Revision buffer adds a percentage on top, because some back-and-forth is normal and should be paid for.
  • Deposit is a share of the total, due before you start. It filters out clients who aren't serious.
  • Extra rounds are priced at the hours each round takes times your rate, so a fifth round isn't free.

Getting the numbers right

Time yourself. Most new editors guess too low. Track your next project from import to export and use that number.

Put the number of revision rounds in writing. A quote that says "2 rounds included, extra rounds $X each" prevents most arguments later. Define a round as one combined list of changes sent together.

Don't hide the buffer. If a project goes smoothly you've earned a fair margin. If it doesn't, the buffer is what keeps you from working for free.
