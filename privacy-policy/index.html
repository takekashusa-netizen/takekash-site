Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TakeKash ("TakeKash," "we," "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you visit takekash.com, use the TakeKash clipping service, or connect a TikTok account to TakeKash. By using TakeKash you agree to the practices described here.

1. Who we are

TakeKash is a video clipping and publishing tool for streamers and creators. It turns long videos (for example, Twitch or YouTube broadcasts) into short clips with captions and, when you choose to, publishes those clips to your own social media accounts, including TikTok. Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@takekash.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact and account details such as your email address, if you create an account or contact us.
  • Content you submit, such as the URL of a video you ask us to process, and the titles, captions, and hashtags you approve for a clip.

Information from TikTok when you connect your account

If you choose to connect a TikTok account through TikTok's official login ("Login Kit"), TikTok asks for your permission and shares only the information covered by the permissions you approve:

TikTok permissionWhat we receive or doWhy
user.info.basicYour TikTok open ID, display name, and avatar imageTo show which TikTok account is connected and to post to the correct account
video.uploadPermission to send a video you selected to your TikTok inbox as a draftSo you can review and post the clip in the TikTok app
video.publishPermission to publish a video you selected, with the caption and settings you choseTo post clips you approved at the time you scheduled

We also receive and store the access token and refresh token TikTok issues after you log in, and the status of each upload (for example, the publish ID and whether the post completed). We do not receive your TikTok password. We do not read your TikTok messages, followers list, or other people's content.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type, and pages visited, recorded in standard server logs by our hosting provider.
  • Processing logs such as when a clip job started and finished and whether it succeeded, used to run and fix the service.

We do not use advertising trackers or sell data to advertisers.

3. How we use information

  • To process the videos you submit into clips, and to generate titles, captions, and hashtags for your review.
  • To upload or publish a clip to your TikTok account only after you have chosen that clip, reviewed its caption and privacy setting, and agreed to post it.
  • To show you the status of your uploads and to prevent the same clip from being posted twice.
  • To keep TikTok access working by refreshing access tokens as TikTok requires.
  • To respond to your requests, provide support, keep the service secure, and meet legal obligations.

We use TikTok data only to provide the features you requested. We do not use it to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell, rent, or trade it.

4. How we share information

  • With TikTok, when you ask us to upload or publish a clip. TikTok's handling of that content is governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy.
  • With service providers who host our website, send email, or process data on our behalf, only as needed to run TakeKash and under obligations to protect it.
  • For legal reasons, if required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users, TakeKash, or others.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

We do not share TikTok data with any other third party for its own purposes.

5. How long we keep information

  • TikTok access and refresh tokens: until you disconnect your TikTok account or they expire. After disconnection we delete them within 30 days.
  • TikTok profile data (open ID, display name, avatar): while your account is connected, and deleted within 30 days after you disconnect.
  • Upload records (which clip was posted and when): up to 12 months, to prevent duplicate posts, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Clips and source files: deleted when no longer needed to provide the service, and in any case on request.
  • Server logs: kept by our hosting provider for its standard retention period.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect TikTok at any time from your TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services permissions → TakeKash → Remove access. You can also email us to disconnect.
  • Access, correct, or delete your information by emailing privacy@takekash.com. We respond within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California, the EU, or the UK), you may have additional rights, such as data portability or objecting to processing. Contact us to use them. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

7. Security

We protect information with reasonable technical and organizational measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) to TikTok and our website, keeping access tokens out of public code and websites, and limiting access to the systems that store them. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

TakeKash is not directed to children under 13 and must not be used by anyone younger than the minimum age required to hold a TikTok account in their country. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International users

TakeKash is operated from the United States. If you use it from another country, your information will be processed in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those in your country.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above and, for material changes, notify you by email or on this website before the change takes effect.
