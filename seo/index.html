Platform rules this tool follows

YouTube & Shorts

Titles can be up to 100 characters, but search shows about 60 to 70 (around 40 in the Shorts feed), so the keyword goes first. Descriptions can be up to 5,000 characters, and the first ~150 are what people see. All tags share a 500-character budget. The first 3 hashtags show above the title, and YouTube ignores hashtags if you use more than 15.

Instagram

Instagram caps every post and Reel at 5 hashtags (since December 2025). We pick a balanced five: your topic, close matches, and one broad category tag.

TikTok

Captions double as search text, so the keyword goes in the first sentence. Use 3 to 5 focused hashtags. Long hashtag walls dilute the topic signal.

Twitch

Titles go up to 140 characters (keep the hook in the first 40 to 60). You get up to 10 tags per stream, each 25 characters max, letters and numbers only, with no spaces. Tags are formatted like this: PlayingWithViewers.

Website / Google

Keep titles around 60 characters and meta descriptions around 140 to 160. Use a short, evergreen URL slug without the year so the page can rank for years.
